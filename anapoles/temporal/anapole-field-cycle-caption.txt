Fields over one optical cycle

Electric and magnetic fields in a 190 × 160 × 100 nm amorphous TiO₂ resonator in air, illuminated at 366.5 nm.

Both panels share one phase clock. Colour shows |E(t)|²/E₀² and |H(t)|²/H₀², including all three vector components, in the x–y plane at z=0 nm. The two colour scales differ, and each remains fixed for the entire cycle.

One optical period, 1.222512 fs, is slowed to 10 seconds. The instantaneous fields are reconstructed directly from the complex vectors with exp(−iωt), using the same time origin.

New independent steady-state Meep reference at the published geometry, not the original Lumerical formation transient.

Geometry: Poeta et al., “Optical anapoles excited by UV-A illumination,” Optics Letters (2024).
https://doi.org/10.1364/OL.536925

Method: Meep FDTD.
https://meep.readthedocs.io/en/latest/

Optical constants: Jolivet et al. (2023).
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apsusc.2022.155214

Equal-length arrows indicate in-plane field direction.
