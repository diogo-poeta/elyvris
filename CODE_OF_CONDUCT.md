# Code of conduct

Elyvris is a small open project in optics and photonics. Everyone is welcome here: students, researchers and developers, at any level of experience, from any background.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are learning, and every question is a fair question.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Give reasons and sources, and accept good reasons from others.
- Give credit. Cite papers, data and code you build on, and name the people who helped.
- Be honest about results. Say whether a number is measured, simulated or assumed.
- Respect privacy. Don't share anyone's personal details or private messages without their consent.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats or personal attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual language or attention.
- Discrimination of any kind, including by gender, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, age or sexual orientation.
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing a topic after being asked to stop.

## Where it applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere someone represents Elyvris.

## Reporting a problem

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, write privately to the maintainer, Diogo Poeta, through [LinkedIn](https://pt.linkedin.com/in/diogo-poeta). Please don't report it in a public issue. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close issues, or block people who break this code. Usually a friendly note comes first.
