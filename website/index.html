01 / COMPUTATIONAL PHOTONICS

FDTD · MEEP
Multipole analysis
Dielectric nanostructures

Shaping electromagnetic fields
with dielectric geometry.

How can a nanostructure control light under UV-A illumination?

My contribution

I designed and simulated dielectric nanostructures using Lumerical FDTD and MEEP, and investigated optical anapoles, field distributions and multipole contributions.

What to inspect

My first-authored paper on UV-A anapole excitation connects electromagnetic modelling with a specific optical design question.

Read the Optics Letters paper ↗
Methods & practical relevance

My modelling work includes electromagnetic field calculations and multipole decomposition. These methods help interpret how geometry changes optical behaviour—a useful basis for nanophotonic design and simulation-led optical R&D. The linked paper is the source for the published findings.

02 / NONLINEAR OPTICS

Thin films · UV optics
Energy velocity
Materials characterisation

Connecting optical design
to nonlinear response.

Can energy velocity help interpret the UV response of TiO₂ thin films?

My contribution

My research combined electromagnetic modelling, energy-velocity analysis, and work on the fabrication and optical characterisation of dielectric thin films.

What to inspect

My first-authored Optical Materials Express paper investigates energy velocity as an optical design indicator for ALD-grown TiO₂ films.

Read the thin-film study ↗
Methods & practical relevance

My wider materials experience includes ellipsometry, UV–Vis–NIR spectroscopy, XRD, XPS and AFM. Connecting measured material properties to optical models helps identify which assumptions matter in a design. The publication reports collaborative research; individual measurements are not assigned here.

03 / EXPERIMENTAL SENSING

Laser instrumentation
Gas handling · LabVIEW
Python analysis

Using ultraviolet light
to distinguish molecules.

How can photoionization reveal which VOCs are in a gas mixture?

My contribution

Within the EU LEARN project, I contributed to selective VOC photoionization sensing. My work included gas handling, laser instrumentation, experimental acquisition and Python analysis.

What to inspect

Our publications describe VUV generation down to 120 nm and real-time molecular-specific BTEX detection. These are team results.

Read the BTEX sensing paper ↗
Methods & practical relevance

I operated gas delivery and flow-control equipment, a femtosecond Yb laser with tunable UV–VIS pulses, and LabVIEW/National Instruments acquisition hardware. This work connects optical experiments to sample handling and analysis in a multidisciplinary sensing project.
