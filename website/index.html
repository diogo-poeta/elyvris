Research

From transparent metal films (2021) to ultraviolet anapoles, tunable vacuum-ultraviolet light for gas sensing and slow light in TiO₂ thin films (2026). Four journal articles and two theses.

The work so far

  1. 2021

    Extraordinary transparency in continuous metallic thin films

    MSc thesis, University of Minho, with the International Iberian Nanotechnology Laboratory. A silver film 30 nm thick normally lets little light through. Placing a patterned layer of a 2D semiconductor (a transition metal dichalcogenide) above and below it raised the simulated transmission to as much as 90%, about ten times the bare film (FDTD simulation). Thesis · PDF · The full story
    Funding acknowledged in the thesis: EU Horizon 2020 Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions · 2D_PHOT · Grant agreement 840064

  2. 2024

    Optical anapoles excited by UV-A illumination

    Optics Letters 49, 7198. Simulations showing that TiO₂ nanostructures can host anapole states, in which the particle scatters very little while it concentrates light inside, at ultraviolet wavelengths. Article

  3. 2025

    Tunable vacuum-ultraviolet light down to 120 nm

    Optics Express 33, 26342. Third-harmonic generation in a suspended SiO₂ nanomembrane produced tunable vacuum-ultraviolet light that ionised volatile organic compounds. Co-author. Article

  4. 2026

    Molecule-specific detection of BTEX gases

    Environmental Technology & Innovation 42, 104895. By scanning the photon energy across ionisation thresholds, the same source distinguished benzene, toluene, ethylbenzene and xylenes in a mixture at occupational exposure limits. Co-author. Article

  5. 2026

    Energy velocity as an optical design indicator

    Optical Materials Express 16, 3342. In ozone-grown TiO₂ films made by atomic layer deposition, a strongly reduced energy velocity (light carrying its energy more slowly) went together with a stronger third-harmonic signal. Article

  6. 2026

    PhD thesis, Bielefeld University

    Tunable VUV photoionization and slow-light enhanced third-harmonic generation in TiO₂ for selective VOC sensing. It connects nonlinear nanophotonics, slow-light design and gas sensing, and points towards compact ultraviolet gas sensors. Thesis · The full story
    Research carried out within EU Horizon Europe · LEARN · Grant agreement 101057510
