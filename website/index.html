UV-A anapole excitation
in TiO₂ nanostructures.

Numerical design of dielectric nanostructures for anapole excitation under UV-A illumination.

My contribution

I designed TiO₂ nanostructures, ran Lumerical FDTD simulations and used MENP for the multipole decomposition of the scattered field.

Publication

The Optics Letters paper analyses UV-A anapole excitation using field distributions and multipole decomposition. Rafael Vilarinho and I contributed equally.

Read the Optics Letters paper ↗
Methods

I calculated electromagnetic fields and decomposed the scattered radiation into multipole contributions to examine the effect of geometry on the anapole condition. The publication reports the numerical results.

NONLINEAR OPTICS

Thin films · UV optics
Energy velocity
Materials characterisation

Energy velocity in
ALD-grown TiO₂ films.

Energy transport in weakly absorbing, dispersive films and its relation to nonlinear response.

My contribution

My research combined electromagnetic modelling, energy-velocity analysis, and work on the fabrication and optical characterisation of dielectric thin films.

Publication

My first-authored Optical Materials Express paper investigates energy velocity as an optical design indicator for ALD-grown TiO₂ films.

Read the thin-film study ↗
Methods

The optical model uses measured material properties. My materials experience includes ellipsometry, UV–Vis–NIR spectroscopy, XRD, XPS and AFM. The publication reports the contributions of the collaborating authors.

Tunable VUV generation
and VOC photoionization.

Selective molecular detection using tunable vacuum ultraviolet light.

My contribution

Within the EU LEARN project, I contributed to selective VOC photoionization sensing. My work included gas handling, laser instrumentation, experimental acquisition and Python analysis.

Publication

The collaborative studies report VUV generation down to 120 nm and real-time molecular-specific BTEX detection.

Read the BTEX sensing paper ↗
Methods

I operated gas-delivery and flow-control equipment, a femtosecond Yb laser with tunable UV–VIS pulses, and LabVIEW/National Instruments acquisition hardware. I acquired and analysed experimental data using Python.
