Project workflow

Python prototype · 29 October 2026

From a physical reference model towards inverse design.

  1. 01

    Inputs

    Layer thicknesses, optical constants, wavelength, angle and polarization. Tabulated n and k are interpolated within their supplied wavelength range.

  2. 02

    Calculate

    The coherent multilayer model calculates reflection R, transmission T and absorption A.

  3. 03

    Validate

    Analytical checks, regression tests and selected comparisons with a separate transfer-matrix calculation.

  4. 04

    Record

    Save the spectrum and optional metadata; document changes, test results and limitations.

Model limits

Planar, isotropic, non-magnetic layers with a lossless incident medium. The substrate may be absorbing. Absorption in the finite layers is calculated as A = 1 − R − T. The checks cover selected cases and do not constitute experimental validation.
